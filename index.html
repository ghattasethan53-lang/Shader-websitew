Installation
Up in 60 seconds.
1
Download the installer
Click the Download button above. Your browser saves install.ps1.
2
Open README.txt first
The installer drops the full shader folder to your Desktop. Open README.txt in that folder before running anything.
3
Run ShaderLoader.exe as Administrator
Right-click → Run as administrator. The loader injects the shader DLLs into Roblox on launch.
⚠ Antivirus may flag the DLL injector — false positive. Add an exception.
4
Launch Roblox
Shaders load automatically. Press F5 to toggle, F6 for settings.
